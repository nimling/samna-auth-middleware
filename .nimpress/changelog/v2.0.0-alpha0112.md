`disciple/sdb` had taken in request bodies, transport names, url and hash helpers and prophet call errors, and `shared.DB` carried disciple calls to the prophet that ran no SQL. Each name now lives in the package `structure.md` gives it. Nothing changes over the wire or in the schema.

## Go

- **breaking** `disciple/sdb` re-exports only the database layer: models, enums, the `*Where` and `Relation*` builders and the access and row errors. `BodyCode`, `BodyClaimGuest`, `BodySamProfileItem`, `BodySamProfileItemKey`, `BodySamUserName`, `UserNameAvailable`, `ErrBadProfileItem`, `ErrBadUserName`, `ErrUserNameTaken`, `GetCodeValue` and `GetCodeHash` are read from `shared`.
- **breaking** `GetProfileLink`, `GetCodeURL`, `CodeParam` and `CodeHeader` are `client`, beside the other prophet urls; `smw.CodeParam` and `smw.CodeHeader` stay.
- **breaking** `GetAppSeed`, `DeleteAppSeed` and `AppSeed` are `client`.
- **breaking** `GetDebugMode`, `DebugMode`, `DebugModeNone`, `DebugModeUser` and `DebugModeClients` are `utils`; `prophet/sdb` no longer re-exports them.
- **breaking** The disciple's `PutProfileItem`, `DeleteProfileItem`, `GetUserNameAvailable` and `PutUserName` are methods of `disciple/sam.Replica`, `sam.Disciple().PutProfileItem(ctx, ac, item)`, with `sam.ErrNoProphet`; they left `shared.DB`. The bodies, their `Validate` and their errors stay in `shared`, where the prophet checks with them.
- **breaking** `AssetResponse`, `AssetCache` and `Options` are `disciple/echo`, registered with `PutOptions`; `disciple/sdb.Init` takes the logger only.
- **breaking** `prophet/sdb.IssueGuestToken` is gone: `prophet/sdb.PutGuestUser` upserts the guest user and answers its grant, and the `PostGuestToken` handler signs the token, `GuestTokenResponse` with it in `prophet/echo`.

## TypeScript

No change.
