The hourly row of alpha0109 asked every app to meter itself to the hour. This release turns `app_cost` into a ledger: the disciple pushes cost rows as they happen, fine or coarse, and the prophet accumulates them. A row covers any window, says what kind of cost it is, carries the app's own reference so a rerun replaces rather than doubles, names the row it corrects, and may carry a schema that checks its evidence and says how it is presented and accumulated. The baseline realigns an alpha0109 table in place.

## The table

`claimius.app_cost` on both roles gains `period_end`, `kind` (`usage`, `fee`, `period`, `credit`), `unit_price`, `ref`, `supersedes_id`, `status` (`estimated`, `settled`, `invoiced`) and `schema`; the hour check on `period_start` goes, a window check `period_end >= period_start` stands, and the live unique index moves from `(app_id, module, period_start)` to `(app_id, module, ref)` on the prophet, `(module, ref)` on a disciple. The baseline adds the columns if they are missing, backfills an existing row with `period_end` one hour on, kind `usage` and the hour as its `ref`, drops the old check and index by name and creates the new one, and reruns clean.

## The write

`POST /sam/cost` takes three forms, the row, the items and the event, and the prophet normalises each into the one row: the amount from `quantity` times `unit_price` when absent, the items summed into the row and stored in `detail.items` with every amount filled, the event a fee at an instant. `201` on a new row, `200` on a replace by ref. A correction names `supersedes_id`, which must be a live row of the app with another ref. `PATCH` takes `status` and `detail` only, `DELETE` soft deletes. A write with an empty `schema` is checked no further; one with a schema has its `detail` validated against that JSON Schema document and refused with the failing path. The middleware ships the default document, `$id` `https://samna.io/schema/cost/1`, accumulating by sum over overlap grouped by `subject.id`, with the rule that its `detail.items` sum to the amount; the items form implies it.

## The read

`claimius.get_app_cost` on both roles takes `p_granularity` `hour`, `day` or `month`, a window `p_from` and `p_to`, and the filters `p_status`, `p_subject_type` and `p_subject_id` read from `detail.subject`. It answers one bucket per module, kind, schema `$id`, group key, unit and currency with the quantity, the amount, the hours folded and the row count. A fee or a credit falls whole into the bucket of its start, usage and period are prorated by overlap, a row some live row supersedes is left out, and the row's `x-samna.accumulate` chooses the method, `sum`, `max`, `last` or `count`, the proration, `overlap` or `start`, and the group paths that make `group_key`.

## Go

- `shared.AppCost` carries the new columns; `shared.AppCostInput` with `AppCostItem` covers the three forms, `ValidatePost` names the first fault and `Normalize` lands the row; `AppCost.Normalize` checks `detail` against `schema`.
- `shared.CostSchema` is the default schema document and `COST_SCHEMA_ID` its `$id`; the kinds, statuses and granularities are constants.
- `shared.GetAppCost(ctx, db, userID, appID, AppCostQuery)` on both roles returns `[]AppCostBucket` with `Kind`, `SchemaID`, `GroupKey`, `Hours` and `Rows`.
- `disciple/sdb.PutAppCost(ctx, db, ac, *AppCostInput)` returns the landed row: routed to the prophet on a deployed disciple, normalised and replaced locally on a debug one.
- The snapshot ships cost rows in creation order so a correction lands after the row it supersedes.

## TypeScript

- `AppCost`, `AppCostInput`, `AppCostItemInput`, `AppCostBucket`, `AppCostQuery`, `CostEvidence`, `CostSchemaDocument`, `CostPresentation`, `CostAccumulate`, `CostKind`, `CostStatus` and `CostGranularity` mirror the Go types; `COST_SCHEMA` and `COST_SCHEMA_ID` the default document.
- `appCostUrl` adds `status`, `subject_type` and `subject_id`; `getAppCost` is unchanged in shape.

## Removed

- The hour check on `period_start` and the unique index on `(app_id, module, period_start)` and `(module, period_start)`.
- `shared.AppCost.ValidatePost`; the input is `AppCostInput`. `ValidatePatch` accepts `status` and `detail` only.
- `GetAppCost`'s positional `module`, `from`, `to`, `granularity` arguments, now `AppCostQuery`; `AppCostBucket.Hours` is the hours of window folded, no longer a row count, which `Rows` carries.
