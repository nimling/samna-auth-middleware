Until now nothing in claimius said what an app costs. This release adds `app_cost`, a routed table on both roles: the app writes its hours through the builder, the prophet stores them and publishes them on the app's feed, and a reader with read on the app gets them back in buckets. Each app decides how it prices itself; claimius carries the count, the unit and the amount.

## The table

`claimius.app_cost` on both roles, registered like the other routed tables: `id`, `app_id` on the prophet, `module`, `period_start` truncated to the hour, `quantity` and `unit`, `amount` and an ISO 4217 `currency`, a free form `detail`, and the `sa_*` columns. An app has one live row per module and hour, `(app_id, module, period_start)` on the prophet and `(module, period_start)` on a disciple, so a second report of an hour updates the row. `module` is the app's own slug for its own usage, or the slug of the built in module that did the work for the app, such as `uptime`, `subscription` or `storage`. Retention never ages it.

## The sync

`app_cost` travels like `claim`, `claim_object` and `user_claim`: the disciple writes it through the builder, the write routes to `POST`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /sam/cost` on the prophet, the prophet stamps the calling app and its organization and checks that the row's grant is a live grant of the actor in that app, and the feed, the snapshot, the peer snapshot and the peer apply carry the row to every replica of the app.

## The read

`claimius.get_app_cost(p_user_id, p_app_id, p_module, p_from, p_to, p_granularity)` on the prophet, the same without the app on a disciple: the live rows summed per module, unit and currency per hour or per UTC day, with the number of hours each bucket folds; `from` inclusive, `to` exclusive, a granularity other than `hour` or `day` refused with `22023`. The prophet answers a reader holding READ on the app through `get_access`, in the app itself or in the prophet's own app; a disciple answers a reader holding READ on the organization owning the rows.

## Go

- `shared.GetAppCost(ctx, db, userID, appID, module, from, to, granularity)` on both roles, returning `[]AppCostBucket`; the app id is ignored on a disciple.
- `disciple/sdb.PutAppCost(ctx, db, ac, row)` upserts one hour through the builder: routed to the prophet on a deployed disciple, a local upsert on a debug one.
- `shared.AppCost` is the model, with `ValidatePost` and `ValidatePatch` for the `/sam` shapes, and `OBJECT_TYPE_APP_COST` the type.

## TypeScript

- `AppCost`, `AppCostBucket`, `AppCostQuery` and `CostGranularity` mirror the Go types, with `OBJECT_TYPE_APP_COST`.
- `getAppCost(url, query)` reads the buckets from whichever route a service exposes on its own `/api`, with the session, and `appCostUrl(url, query)` builds that route's query; the route itself is the service's work.
